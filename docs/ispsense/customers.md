## Create and find a customer

Open Billing → Customers and add a subscriber. Save a clear name and any contact details needed by your business. An initial MAC address is optional; you can register devices later.

The generated customer number is a stable directory reference. A phone number can be shared by multiple records and does not replace the customer number, service username, or network credentials.

## Use the customer profile

Open a customer to inspect their overview, access periods, management controls, payments, sessions, and changes. Use Manage access for devices, connection limits, schedules, provisioning history, and alerts.

Complimentary access and speed overrides are available from the profile. Review the requested and effective state separately: a saved change may still be waiting for a router adapter.

## Devices and connection limits

Register known device MAC addresses and labels. You can disable a registered device and configure the customer-wide device and simultaneous-session limits. Registered-only access is optional.

MAC registration helps enforce identity policy once the network integration is ready. Registering a MAC does not itself authorize internet access, and a MAC address should not be treated as a secret.

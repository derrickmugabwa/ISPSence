## Quick start

Start with your business settings, an internet package, and a customer. You can prepare these records before connecting a router or configuring a payment provider.

The dashboard and assistant read the same saved database records. A pending request describes what should happen; it becomes effective only after the appropriate network confirmation.

## Setup checklist

1. **Review your settings.** Open Settings and check the options available for your installation. Configure integrations only when you have their credentials.
2. **Create your first package.** Open Billing → Packages. Choose its service types, price, duration, speeds, and optional data allowance.
3. **Add a customer.** Open Billing → Customers. Save their contact details; register a device MAC if you already know it.
4. **Prepare access.** Open the customer profile to manage services, complimentary access, schedules, and connection limits.
5. **Check readiness.** Review provisioning status before telling a customer that their connection is available.

## What you can do now

Manage customer and package records, configure access policies, record offline payments, customize portal templates, and configure AI connections. The background pipeline retains provisioning work and accounting observations across restarts.

Production router enforcement, effective paid activation, and automatic FUP application still require a qualified router integration. PayHero's real payment flow requires provider credentials and verification. See [Feature readiness](/docs/feature-readiness) before a rollout.

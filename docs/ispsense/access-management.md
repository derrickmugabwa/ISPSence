## Complimentary access

Use Complimentary access on the customer profile to grant a finite package-backed period without payment. Provide a reason and review the duration. Revoke the grant when it should no longer be eligible.

The grant has its own expiry and audit history. Creating it does not fabricate router readiness or a payment receipt.

## Speed overrides

Use Speed override to save customer-specific download and upload limits. Reset the override to return to package defaults. Review provisioning history to distinguish the requested policy from network confirmation.

## Suspend and resume

Manage access → Controls allows suspension and resumption. Suspension restricts access while purchased time continues. It is suitable for an administrative restriction that should not preserve time.

## Pause and vacation

Pause/vacation is intended to preserve remaining time. Preservation requires a confirmed disconnection; clicking Pause alone is not proof that access stopped. Resume also waits for access readiness and schedule eligibility.

The original period timestamps remain in history. A confirmed runtime adjustment records the preserved duration and adjusted expiry. Production application still waits for the qualified adapter.

## Alerts and audit history

Review upcoming expiry, expired access, and provisioning-failure notices under Billing → Access alerts or on the customer profile. Acknowledging a notice marks it read; it does not resolve its cause. Current alerts are in-dashboard; SMS and email delivery are not claimed.

## What is ISPSense?

ISPSense brings customer billing, access management, and MikroTik network operations into one dashboard. It builds on MikroTik Manager and adds the billing, customer service, portal, payment integration, and AI features documented here.

## Find your workspace

- **Dashboard:** collections, customer access, and records needing attention, alongside network summary and operations tabs.
- **Billing:** packages, customers, payments, subscriptions, access accounts, captive portals, diagnostics, alerts, and sessions.
- **Network and monitoring:** devices, traffic, events, and network services.
- **Settings:** application configuration, PayHero, and AI connections.

## Understand the records

A customer identifies a subscriber. A package describes an offer. A payment records the purchase and its payment history. A subscription represents an access period, while a service identifies the customer's connection.

Customer numbers help staff find records; they are not passwords or proof of payment. An open session is a saved observation and may be stale. Configuration, funding, provisioning, and current connectivity are separate facts.

## Before serving customers

Read [Feature readiness](/docs/feature-readiness). The application currently keeps live authorization blocked until router admission and access-readiness checks are qualified. You can configure the business while that integration is being completed.
